How it works

  • A dual-head CNN predicts a move policy and a win-probability estimate directly from the raw board.
  • PUCT Monte Carlo Tree Search uses those predictions to look ahead and refine the move choice.
  • The network was trained purely through self-play — no human games, no hand-coded strategy.
